about=A redesigned Mapflow plugin: one flow from what to map to results on the map, in a dock next to it.
    What you can do:
    - Start: run a processing. Pick a scenario or a model, set the area from a layer, a selection, a drawing or the map view, choose imagery, check the cost and run.
    - Start: map your own imagery. Upload a GeoTIFF, or use a My imagery collection or one image in it.
    - Imagery sources: search the satellite imagery catalog by area, date and cloud cover, and use an image.
    - Processings: follow progress, filter and search the history, rename, run again with the same settings.
    - Results load as styled layers (GeoPackage or vector tiles), or save as GeoJSON.
    - Projects: switch, search and pin projects, including shared ones.
    Managing My imagery collections, projects and sharing, reviews and your balance happens in Mapflow Web (https://app.mapflow.ai); the plugin links to each page.
    Requires a Mapflow account (250 free credits to start): https://app.mapflow.ai
